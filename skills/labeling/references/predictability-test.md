# Predictability test

Read when planning how to check labels with people, or analysing results someone brings back.

It is a small qualitative check that can be run in a call or an online form. Use 5 to 10 people who match the audience, per audience. Report what they said. With this few people a percentage is decoration.

## Two directions

**Label to expectation.** Show one label at a time, without the page. "What do you expect to find if you choose this?"

**Goal to label.** Show the sibling labels together and give a goal in the person's words, never using the label's own words. "You want to get your money back for a duplicate payment. Which would you choose?" A task that repeats the label tests reading, not understanding.

## Plan

```
Labels under test: <group, label, what is really behind it>
Who: <audience, how many, how recruited>
Part 1, expectation: <labels shown one at a time, in random order>
Part 2, goal: <goals in the participants' words, with the label each should lead to>
Competing candidates: <show one candidate per person, or ask which of two they would choose and why>
Decision it feeds: <what changes based on the result>
```

## Reading the results

| What you see | It means | Do |
|---|---|---|
| People describe what is really there, in their own words | The label works | Keep it, and note their words for descriptions |
| Answers are varied or hedged ("could be anything") | The label is vague | Replace it with something more specific |
| Answers are confident and wrong | The label promises something else | Rename it urgently; this is worse than vague |
| People use a different word for the right thing | You have found their word | Try it as the label or a synonym |
| A goal splits between two siblings | Those two overlap | Reword both, or fix the grouping |
| One audience gets it and another does not | The word is a term of art | Decide who the label is for, or add a gloss |

## Results record

```
Label | Said they expected (their words) | Correct? | Goal test: chose | Verdict
```

Keep the words people used. They are the most useful output, and they feed the search synonyms and the descriptions.

When no people are available, deliver this plan with the labels marked `untested`. Do not fill in the record yourself.
