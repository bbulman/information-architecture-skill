Our project management app has 18 items in the left sidebar, in this order: Dashboard, My Tasks, Projects, Timeline, Calendar, Reports, Analytics, Insights, Files, Docs, Wiki, Team, People, Integrations, Billing, Settings, Help, What's New. Support tickets keep coming in from people who can't find where to export a report or where to invite a teammate. Our users are mostly project managers at agencies with 10 to 50 staff. Propose a better structure for the sidebar and write it up in nav-proposal.md.
